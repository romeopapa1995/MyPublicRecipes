Pear and Walnut Salad

Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 2 pears, sliced
  • 1/2 cup walnuts
  • 1/4 cup feta or goat cheese (optional)
  • 1/4 cup dried cranberries (optional)
  • 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Whisk dressing ingredients together.
  2. Combine greens, pears, walnuts, cheese and cranberries.
  3. Toss with dressing.
