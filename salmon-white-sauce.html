Salmon with White Sauce

Ingredients

  • 250 g fish or salmon
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1/2 cup celery
  • 1/2 cup carrot
  • 10 g butter
  • 1 tsp flour
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 potato
  • 1 tsp parsley

Instructions

  1. Cook onion in oil.
  2. Add fish and cook.
  3. Add celery, carrot and parsley; simmer.
  4. Make white sauce with butter, flour and milk.
  5. Cook and mash potato.
  6. Serve fish on mash with white sauce.
