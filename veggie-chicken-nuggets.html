Vegetable Loaded Chicken Nuggets

Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup peas
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 zucchini, grated
  • 500 g chicken mince
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 220 g panko crumbs
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 3 eggs, whisked

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C.
  2. Toast panko crumbs.
  3. Blend vegetables, eggs, salt and garlic powder.
  4. Fold in chicken mince.
  5. Shape nuggets.
  6. Crumb: flour → egg → panko.
  7. Bake 25–30 minutes.
