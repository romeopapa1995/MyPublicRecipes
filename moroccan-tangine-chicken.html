Moroccan Chicken Tagine

Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup dried apricots
  • 8 chicken drumsticks
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ginger
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 400 g chickpeas
  • 400 g diced tomatoes
  • 2 cups bone broth

Instructions

  1. Soak apricots in boiling water.
  2. Brown chicken in oil; remove.
  3. Sauté onion and garlic.
  4. Add spices; cook 1–2 minutes.
  5. Add broth, apricots, chickpeas and tomatoes.
  6. Return chicken; simmer 30 minutes.
