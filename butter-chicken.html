Butter Chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 7.5 cm ginger
  • 50 g cashews
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 150 g yoghurt
  • 4 chicken breasts, cubed
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 2 long green chillies
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 75 g chilled butter
  • 50 ml cream
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Blend spices, garlic, ginger, cashews and lemon juice.
  2. Stir in yoghurt.
  3. Marinate chicken for 1 hour.
  4. Grill chicken 12–15 minutes.
  5. Fry cinnamon stick and cardamom pods.
  6. Add garlic, chillies and tomatoes; simmer 4–5 minutes.
  7. Add grilled chicken; cook 4–5 minutes.
  8. Whisk in butter, add cream, simmer 2 minutes.
