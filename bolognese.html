Bolognese

Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 500g beef mince
  • 2 tins diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp Kalamata olives, sliced
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp parsley
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp tarragon (optional)
  • Pasta of choice
  • Parmesan cheese, to serve
  • Pinch of sugar (optional)

Method

  1. Heat a large pan over medium heat. Add a splash of oil and cook the diced onion until soft and translucent.
  2. Add the beef mince and cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned.
  3. Stir in the diced tomatoes and tomato paste. If using, add a splash of red wine to rinse out the tins into the pan.
  4. Add the olives, oregano, parsley, thyme and tarragon. Mix well.
  5. Simmer on medium heat for 10–15 minutes, allowing the sauce to reduce and thicken. Add a pinch of sugar if desired to balance acidity.
  6. Meanwhile, cook pasta in boiling salted water according to packet instructions.
  7. Drain pasta and serve topped with the bolognese sauce and grated parmesan.
