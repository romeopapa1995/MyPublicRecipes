Bolognese

Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 500 g beef mince
  • 2 tins diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp Kalamata olives
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp parsley
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp tarragon (optional)
  • Pasta
  • Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Fry onion until soft.
  2. Add mince and brown.
  3. Add tomatoes and paste; simmer.
  4. Add olives and spices.
  5. Cook pasta.
  6. Serve with sauce and parmesan.
